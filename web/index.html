How to play

Four secret 5-letter words form a square — top, bottom, left and right — sharing their corner letters.

Guess any valid 5-letter word. Every letter in your guess is revealed everywhere it appears in the square, hangman-style.

  • ❤️ You can survive only 3 wrong guesses. Naming one of the four words exactly is a free guess.
  • 🎯 Solve words early for more points: 100 → 80 → 60 → 40 → 30 → 20 → 10 by guess number. Naming a word exactly beats letting it auto-reveal (−10).
  • 💡 Hints reveal a random letter but cost 15 points (max 3 per game) — and a word finished off by a hint earns no points.
  • 🏅 Win bonus: +25 points per unused wrong guess.
  • 🌍 The 🏆 leaderboard is global — every player competes on the same board.

Daily gives everyone the same puzzle each day — one scored attempt. Random is unlimited free play.
